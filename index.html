Bins & Risk

How often do BINs get banned, and what happens to my balance?

BINs can burn over time. That's the nature of this market. When a BIN starts showing unhealthy decline patterns, we rotate it before it hits your spend. Your balance stays on your account, and you can move to new cards from a fresh BIN without losing funds.

What happens if my card gets flagged in a Meta risk payment review?

Message your manager in Telegram as soon as it happens. We can't control what Meta decides internally, but we know the common patterns and can guide you on the next best step based on your account setup and current situation.

Do you share current link performance for Meta and Google?

Stable performance on Meta, Google and TikTok across our active BIN families. Exact current numbers vary daily and are shared by manager in chat — they depend on geo, vertical and account quality.

Do I need to manage old cards myself?

Yes. When you stop using a card, delete or unlink it from your ad account and remove it from your Papa Bin dashboard. If you leave inactive cards attached to ad accounts, they keep getting charged or declined — this raises your decline rate and hurts the link performance of your next cards. Card hygiene is on you, not on the platform.

How long do cards stay active?

Cards have an expiration date set when issued. Active cards work until expiration or until you delete them. We don't auto-close cards unless they violate usage rules.

What if my card details get compromised?

Block the card in your dashboard immediately. Cards are virtual, so loss usually means leaked details, not physical theft.

Money & Funding

USDT top-up: 2.9%. Wire and other methods — separate terms, discussed with the manager based on your volume.

Which USDT networks do you support?

TRC20 and ERC20. TRC20 is faster and cheaper, so most teams use it.

What is the minimum deposit?

$500 minimum. If you want to start smaller for testing, mention it to the manager — flexibility depends on your setup.

Can I withdraw an unused balance?

Yes, via USDT. Processed within 24 hours. A processing fee applies — the manager confirms the exact amount before the transfer.

Team Operations

How do I add a buyer to my team?

You add buyers yourself in the dashboard. Three roles available: owner, team lead, buyer. Each role has its own permissions, spend visibility and limits. Onboarding a new buyer takes minutes.

Can I remove a buyer at any time?

Yes, anytime. Remove a buyer from your dashboard — access is cut immediately. Other buyers and cards keep working without interruption.

Can I set spending limits per buyer?

Yes. Set per-buyer daily limits, per-card limits, or both. Limits are enforced in real time — if a buyer hits the cap, the card stops.

Trust & Onboarding

What information do you need from me to start?

Basic details about you and your team — team name, contact, what you run, and approximate monthly spend.

What are the card usage restrictions?

Papa Bin cards are built for legitimate ad spend and ad-related tools. Some operations are prohibited and will lead to account closure without balance refund. Full list on our Terms of Use page.

If you're not sure your use case is allowed, ask the manager before top up.
